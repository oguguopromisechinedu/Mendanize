# Mendanize Engineering Specification (MES-005)
## Premium Homepage Experience

**Version:** 2.0 (renumbered from original MES-004)
**Dependencies:** MES-002, MES-003, MES-004, MSEM Appendix A

Follows MSEM Appendix A. No exceptions.

---

## OBJECTIVE
Build a world-class homepage communicating what Mendanize is, who it's for, why it's different. Feels like premium SaaS, not a traditional blog. All sections reusable and admin-manageable (MES-012).

---

## SECTIONS

1. **Premium Hero** — headline, description, primary/secondary CTA, illustration area, background gradients, floating UI elements, trust indicators.
2. **Ask Mendanize AI** — public Tier 1 widget per MES-018 v2: search field, suggested/popular questions, voice search placeholder. Interface only — real AI Service wiring is MES-018's later phase.
3. **Platform Statistics** — Articles, Guides, AI Tools, Categories, Readers — animated placeholders, real values from Analytics (MES-022) later.
4. **Featured Learning Categories** — responsive card grid: image, icon, title, description, article count, hover animation. Pulled via Content Service (MES-002).
5. **Learning Paths** — cards: cover, difficulty, duration, lessons, progress placeholder, CTA.
6. **Featured Articles** — modern article cards: image, category, title, description, reading time, author, date, bookmark placeholder.
7. **Featured AI Tools** — logo, name, description, category, rating placeholder, Learn More.
8. **Why Learn With Mendanize** — feature cards: Expert Content, AI Assistance, Structured Learning, Always Updated, Real Skills.
9. **Testimonials** — cards, prepared for future DB integration.
10. **Newsletter** — headline, description, input, button, privacy message.
11. **FAQ** — accessible accordion.
12. **Final CTA** — strong closing section.

All section content/visibility/order is admin-manageable via MES-012 (Homepage CMS) — nothing hardcoded.

---

## WHAT NOT TO BUILD
Authentication, Dashboard, real AI functionality, Article/Learning pages, real search logic, backend APIs beyond placeholder data.

---

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
✓ Homepage feels premium, every section reusable, fully responsive, ready for MES-012 CMS integration and database integration.

---

Summarize what was built. Wait for MES-006.
