# Mendanize Engineering Specification (MES-025)
## Public Article Experience

**Version:** 1.0
**Dependencies:** MES-002, MES-004, MES-008, MES-015 (SEO), MES-018 (Recommendations), MES-019 (Ask Mendanize AI), MSEM Appendix A

Follows MSEM Appendix A — including its SEO Standard, in full, since this is a primary public surface. No exceptions.

---

## OBJECTIVE
Build the public-facing Article reading experience — premium, readable, discoverable, and connected into the rest of the platform rather than ending when the article ends.

---

## BUILD
Article Page: Header, Reading Progress Bar, Sticky Table of Contents, Body, Author Section, Related Content, Ask Mendanize AI widget, Share Section, Footer. Public Website Layout (MES-004).

## HEADER / READING EXPERIENCE
Featured Image, Category, Topic, Title, Description, Author, Dates, Reading Time, Difficulty/Featured badges. Progress bar, sticky TOC, responsive typography, optimized line length, code blocks, tables, callouts.

## BODY
Renders headings, paragraphs, lists, images, code, tables, quotes, internal/external links, callouts — via reusable content components (MES-003).

## AUTHOR SECTION
Avatar, name, bio, social placeholder, article count placeholder.

## RELATED CONTENT
Related Articles/Guides/Tools/Topics/Categories — calls the Recommendations Service (MES-018) exclusively, no ad hoc logic.

## ASK MENDANIZE AI
Embeds the **Tier 1 public contextual widget** from MES-019 — "Ask about this topic," scoped to this article's content, no login required.

## SHARING & FOOTER
Copy Link, Share/Print/Bookmark placeholders. Footer: Tags, Next/Previous Article, Continue Learning CTA, Newsletter CTA.

## SEO
Full Appendix A SEO Standard: structured data (Article schema), canonical URLs, OG/Twitter Cards, breadcrumbs — via the SEO Service (MES-015).

---

## WHAT NOT TO BUILD
Comments, ratings, real reading-progress persistence, real sharing APIs, reactions.

---

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
✓ Article page, reading experience, TOC, related content, Ask Mendanize AI widget, SEO structure all exist. Zero build/TS/lint errors.

---

## STOP
Summarize the architecture. Explain integration with Guides, AI Tools, Categories, Topics, Search, SEO, and Ask Mendanize AI. Wait for MES-026.
