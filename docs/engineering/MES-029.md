# Mendanize Engineering Specification (MES-029)

## Final Quality Assurance, Testing & Production Launch

**Version:** 1.1 — expanded to cover MES-031–035, which didn't exist when this spec was first written and were previously missing from its scope entirely. **Dependencies:** All prior specs (MES-001–028), MES-030 (Dual Authentication & Authorization Architecture), MES-031 (AI Knowledge Generation Pipeline), MES-032 (Observability & Logging), MES-033 (Caching & Performance), MES-034 (Backup & Recovery), MES-035 (Privacy & Compliance Basics)

No major new features are introduced in this phase.

---

## OBJECTIVE

Verify Mendanize meets MSEM standards end-to-end and confirm it's genuinely production-ready — not just that MES-028's audit found and fixed issues, but that the whole platform works as an integrated system.

---

## FUNCTIONAL TESTING

Verify every workflow across every module functions correctly end-to-end: sign-up → email verification → sign-in; article creation → AI-assisted draft (MES-011) → SEO (MES-015) → publish → appears on public site (MES-025) → appears in search (MES-017) → appears in related content (MES-018); guide authoring → public guide experience; AI tool curation → public directory; subscription checkout → dashboard reflects tier → feature gating (once defined) responds correctly.

## CROSS-MODULE INTEGRATION CHECKS

Specifically verify the seams that were the whole point of this restructure:

- Recommendations Service (MES-018) is the only source of "related content" anywhere it appears (MES-017, MES-022, MES-023, MES-025–027) — no module has its own copy.
- AI Settings (MES-020) is the only AI configuration screen — MES-011 and MES-019 both link to it, neither redefines it.
- Ask Mendanize AI's Tier 1/Tier 2 handoff (MES-019) actually works from each public content page into the full Tier 2 experience, authenticated as a `PublicUser` — this is not the Admin Dashboard and must never accept an `Admin` session.
- Dual Authentication (MES-030) holds under test: a `PublicUser` session cannot reach any `/dashboard/*` route, an `Admin` session cannot reach `PublicUser`-only areas (My Learning, Saved Content), and the dashboard sign-in page shows no registration/social/magic-link options.
- MES-031's one-way trigger holds under test: submitting a question that has no existing answer actually queues an AI Draft, and no `PublicUser` session — including the one that asked the triggering question — can see, list, or reach that draft or the AI Knowledge Center.
- Account deletion (MES-035) actually cascades correctly: a deleted `PublicUser`'s saved content (MES-022), subscription (MES-021, canceled first), and Ask Mendanize AI conversation history (MES-019) are all removed or anonymized — no orphaned records left behind.
- Navigation (MES-016) actually drives the header/footer built in MES-004 — nothing hardcoded remains.
- Design Customization (MES-003/MES-020) actually drives visual settings platform-wide — spot-check that changing a brand color in the dashboard propagates across all three surfaces (Teaching Frontend, Learner Account Area, Admin Dashboard).
