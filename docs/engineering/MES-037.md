**Version:** 1.0 — correctly numbered MES-037 (the pasted draft said MES-031, already taken by the AI Knowledge Generation Pipeline; MES-037 was free — the original "Observability Platform" that once held this slot was rightsized as MES-032 instead). **Dependencies:** MES-002 (Shared Services — AI Service for insights, Audit Service for logging), MES-006/MES-030 (Dual Authentication — gated to the **Super Administrator** role specifically, the strictest of MES-030's existing roles, not a new one), MES-007 (Admin Dashboard — new Business Intelligence domain), MES-021 (Billing — revenue inputs), MES-023 (Analytics & Insights Platform — **reused, not duplicated**, see below), MES-039 (Marketplace/Workforce data — reused for marketplace and job metrics), MSEM Appendix A

## WHAT THIS IS AND ISN'T

This is an internal heuristic estimate, not an accounting valuation. It should never be presented — internally or externally — as a real business valuation an investor, lender, or accountant would rely on. Keep the UI's own language honest about that (the original draft's "Confidence: Medium" label is good practice — keep it, and keep the number itself visually distinct from anything resembling audited financials).

---

## THE MAIN CORRECTION: THIS DOESN'T REBUILD MES-023

The original draft's Revenue Analytics, Marketplace Analytics, Workforce Analytics, Learning Analytics, and Traffic Analytics sections would, read literally, recreate most of what **MES-023 (Analytics & Insights Platform)** already owns — Content/Learning/AI/Search/User/Traffic Analytics, all backed by one shared `AnalyticsEvent` stream. Rebuilding five analytics dashboards a second time here is exactly the duplication this whole spec sequence has been fixing at every turn.

What this spec actually adds that's genuinely new:

- **The Valuation Engine** — a computed heuristic number, which doesn't exist anywhere else.
- **Valuation History** — an immutable log of that number over time.
- **Growth/AI Insights** — natural-language summaries of the numbers, via the existing AI Service (MES-002), not a new AI system.
- **Investor View** — a curated presentation layer over data that already exists.

Everything under "Platform Metrics" below is **read from MES-023 (traffic, learning, content, search), MES-021 (revenue, MRR/ARR, churn), and MES-039 (marketplace transactions, jobs, developers/creators)** — this page aggregates them into one Super-Admin view, it does not reimplement any of them.

**Terminology correction:** "Workers/Employers/Developers" are corrected to match MES-039's actual model — a `PublicUser` with a Client flag (posts jobs), a `PublicUser` fulfilling a `Contract` (does the work), and a `PublicUser` with a Creator flag (sells AI Tools Marketplace listings). "Course Enrollments" corrects to Learning Guide starts (MES-010).

## NEW DASHBOARD DOMAIN: BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

Added to MES-007: Founder Dashboard, Company Valuation, Growth Analytics, Revenue Analytics, Investor Metrics — all Super-Administrator-gated, distinct from every other MES-007 domain (which are visible to the broader `Admin` roles per their permissions).

## COMPANY VALUATION PAGE

Large "Estimated Value" card (dollar figure, growth %, confidence label, last-updated timestamp) — the one genuinely new visual centerpiece.

## PLATFORM METRICS (read-only aggregation, not reimplementation)

Total Users, Active Users (30 days), Premium Subscribers — from MES-021/MES-006. Learning Guide starts, Certificates Issued — from MES-010/MES-039. Published Articles, AI-Generated Articles — from MES-008/MES-031. AI Tools Marketplace listings, purchases, Creators — from MES-039. Job postings, contracts completed, Clients — from MES-039. Monthly/Annual Revenue, MRR, ARR, Churn, Retention, CLV — from MES-021. Website Visitors, Page Views, Search Traffic, Returning Users — from MES-023. Every number is a live read from its owning spec's data — never hardcoded, never a parallel copy.

## VALUATION ENGINE

A backend component (owned by this spec, not a new platform-wide shared service — nothing else will ever call into it) that takes revenue, MRR/ARR, user/subscriber/marketplace/AI-tool growth, engagement, traffic, and retention as inputs and produces one estimated figure. Store each input factor separately (a `ValuationFactor` per calculation), so the weighting formula can change later without losing history. **Start with a simple, clearly-labeled heuristic** (e.g., an ARR multiple adjusted by growth and retention modifiers) — the exact formula is a business decision to make deliberately, not something to hardcode here as if it were authoritative.

## GROWTH HISTORY

Every calculation is saved, never overwritten — `ValuationSnapshot` rows, queryable daily/weekly/monthly/yearly, charted.

## GROWTH / AI INSIGHTS

Natural-language summaries ("user growth increased 23%," "retention improved") generated by the existing AI Service (MES-002) — the same provider infrastructure as Ask Mendanize AI, not a second AI system. Inputs are the computed metrics above, not free-form data.

## INVESTOR VIEW

A curated summary page (estimated worth, growth/revenue trends, users, subscribers, retention) over already-existing data. PDF export is explicitly future — don't build it now.

---

## DATABASE

`ValuationSnapshot (date, estimatedValue, growthPercent, confidenceLevel), ValuationFactor (snapshotId, factorName, factorValue), GrowthSnapshot (date range, aggregated metrics)`. Every write is Super-Administrator-only and Audit-logged (MES-002) — this is sensitive internal data, treated with the same rigor as any other `Admin` action.

---

## SECURITY

Super Administrator role only, every route protected, every valuation calculation and manual refresh audit-logged. No `PublicUser` session has any path to this data, ever — there's no boundary-crossing question here at all, unlike MES-036/MES-039, since nothing here is learner-facing.

---

## WHAT NOT TO BUILD

PDF export (future). Third-party analytics integrations (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, PostHog, Microsoft Clarity, additional payment processors) — architecture should allow adding these later without redesign, but none get wired up now. Any ML-based valuation prediction — rules-based heuristic only, same "simple first" discipline as Recommendations (MES-018) and Career Readiness (MES-039).
