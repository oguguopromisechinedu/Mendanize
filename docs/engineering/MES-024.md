# Mendanize Engineering Specification (MES-024)
## Notification & Communication System

**Version:** 1.0
**Dependencies:** MES-002 (Shared Services — Notification Service), MES-006 (Auth), MES-020 (Platform Settings), MSEM Appendix A

Follows MSEM Appendix A. No exceptions.

---

## OBJECTIVE
Build the single system every platform communication routes through — user/admin notifications, announcements, email templates — via the Notification Service (MES-002), so no module invents its own notification logic.

---

## BUILD
Pages: Notifications Dashboard, Notification Center, Notification Templates, Email Templates, Announcements, Communication History, Delivery Settings, Notification Preferences. Admin Dashboard Layout (MES-007).

## NOTIFICATION CENTER
View/search/filter/sort, mark read/unread, archive, delete. Columns: title, preview, type, priority, status, date.

## NOTIFICATION TYPES
Information, Success, Warning, Error, Security, System, Learning, AI, Announcement — each with icon, color theme, priority.

## EMAIL TEMPLATES
Welcome, Password Reset, Email Verification (these three are triggered by MES-006's auth flows through this service), Account Updates, Security Alerts, Learning Reminder, Newsletter, Platform Announcement.

## ANNOUNCEMENTS
Platform Announcements, Maintenance Notices (ties to MES-020's Maintenance Mode), Feature Releases, Learning Campaigns.

## COMMUNICATION HISTORY / DELIVERY SETTINGS
Log with search/filter/pagination. Delivery channel toggles: In-App, Email, Browser/Push/SMS placeholders.

## USER NOTIFICATION PREFERENCES
Learning Updates, AI Updates, Security Alerts, Newsletter, Product Updates, Announcements — per-user, exposed in MES-022's Account Preferences.

## DATABASE
Models: `Notification, NotificationType, NotificationPreference, NotificationTemplate, EmailTemplate, Announcement, CommunicationLog, DeliverySetting`.

---

## WHAT NOT TO BUILD
Actual email delivery (SMTP wiring — placeholder per MES-020), push/SMS delivery, real-time notifications, third-party messaging integration.

---

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
✓ All notification pages, types, templates, announcements, history, and preferences exist as the single canonical system. Zero build/TS/lint errors.

---

## STOP
Summarize the architecture. Explain how Auth, content modules, Billing, and future modules route through it. Wait for MES-025.
