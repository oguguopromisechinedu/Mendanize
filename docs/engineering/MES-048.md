# Mendanize Engineering Specification (MES-048)

## Marketplace Dispute Resolution Workflow

**Version:** 1.0 — human-reviewed dispute process deferred by MES-039 (no automated dispute bots).  
**Status:** Complete  
**Dependencies:** MES-002 (Audit, Notification), MES-007 (Marketplace domain), MES-021 (untouched), MES-024, MES-039 (Contracts, Milestones, Stripe Connect), MES-040 (optional org party), MSEM Appendix A

Follows MSEM Appendix A. No exceptions.

---

## OBJECTIVE

Give Clients and Workers a structured path to open a dispute on a `Contract` / `Milestone`, collect evidence, and let Admins resolve it — including coordination with Stripe Connect where funds are held — without pretending AI can adjudicate money fairly.

---

## FLOW

1. Participant opens `Dispute` (reason codes: non-payment, scope, quality, other).  
2. Both parties submit statements + attachments (Media Library).  
3. Status: `OPEN` → `UNDER_REVIEW` → `RESOLVED` | `REJECTED` | `WITHDRAWN`.  
4. Admin resolution notes + action: release milestone, partial refund instruction, cancel contract flag — **money movement still goes through Stripe Connect APIs already used by MES-039**, not a parallel ledger.  
5. Notifications to parties (MES-024). Full Admin audit trail.

Surfaces: `/account/hiring/disputes`, `/account/...` worker view, `/dashboard/marketplace/disputes`.

---

## WHAT NOT TO BUILD

Automated escrow AI judges. Public court-of-opinion forums. Chargeback management platforms beyond what Stripe already surfaces. Tax/legal advice engines.

---

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

✓ Either party can open a dispute on an eligible contract. ✓ Admin can resolve with an audit log. ✓ Resolution actions call existing Connect payment paths only. ✓ No Admin session crossover into learner impersonation. Zero build/TS/lint errors.
