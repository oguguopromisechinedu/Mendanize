# Mendanize Engineering Specification (MES-035)
## Privacy & Compliance Basics

**Version:** 1.0 — rightsized from the pasted "Enterprise Governance, Compliance, Privacy & Data Lifecycle Platform" (MES-053). Kept: the parts that are genuine legal obligations if any `PublicUser` is in the EU/California — consent, data export, account deletion, a real privacy policy. Dropped: Legal Hold Service, Risk Assessment Service, ISO 27001/SOC 2 readiness, ISO-style Governance Center, ten named governance services — all enterprise-compliance-team scale, not relevant until Mendanize has enterprise contracts requiring them.
**Dependencies:** MES-002 (Shared Services — Audit Service), MES-006/MES-030 (Dual Authentication — this spec applies to `PublicUser` accounts primarily), MSEM Appendix A

Follows MSEM Appendix A. No exceptions.

---

## OBJECTIVE
Cover the real, non-optional privacy obligations a public-facing platform with EU/California users has — nothing more, nothing enterprise-theater. If Mendanize later signs enterprise customers who need SOC 2 or ISO 27001, that's a real future spec, written when it's actually needed.

---

## CONSENT
A cookie/analytics consent banner on first visit (public site), stored per-session/per-account. Newsletter and marketing consent captured explicitly at signup (already implied by MES-021's/MES-006's account flows) — not bundled into a blanket "I agree to everything" checkbox.

## DATA EXPORT
A `PublicUser` can request an export of their own account data (profile, saved content, learning history per MES-022) in JSON. This is a genuine GDPR/CCPA right-to-access requirement, not optional.

## ACCOUNT DELETION
A `PublicUser` can delete their account, which removes/anonymizes their personal data per the account's own request — this is a real legal requirement, distinct from an `Admin` deactivating an account. Deletion triggers cascade cleanup across MES-022's saved-content models, MES-021's subscription (canceled first), and MES-019's conversation history.

## PRIVACY POLICY
A real, versioned Privacy Policy page (public, static content) — not a governance dashboard, just an accurate page describing what data is collected and why.

## AUDIT TRAIL FOR PRIVACY ACTIONS
Data export requests and account deletions are logged via the Audit Service (MES-002) — who requested it, when, and confirmation it completed. This is the only "governance" infrastructure actually needed here.

---

## WHAT NOT TO BUILD
Legal Hold Service, Risk Assessment Service, data classification taxonomy, retention-policy configuration UI, ISO 27001/SOC 2 compliance program, a dedicated "Governance Center" dashboard, ten named governance microservices. These are real things large enterprises build — Mendanize doesn't need them at this stage, and building them now is pure overhead with no user or business benefit.

---

## ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
✓ Consent banner exists and is stored per-account. ✓ Data export works and produces accurate JSON. ✓ Account deletion actually cascades correctly across saved content, subscriptions, and conversations. ✓ Privacy Policy page exists and is accurate. ✓ Export/deletion actions are audit-logged. Zero build/TS/lint errors.

---

## STOP
Summarize completion. Confirm deletion cascades correctly and doesn't leave orphaned records in Personalization (MES-022), Billing (MES-021), or Ask Mendanize AI (MES-019). Wait for next spec.
